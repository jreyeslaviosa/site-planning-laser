Artwork assumptions

Panel size and four sections follow the supplied PARALLELS rider. Placement is a proposal. Laser distance and mounting height are editable assumptions; the detailed rider drawings describe the eight-laser version.

The purple envelope adds 0.6 m at each side, 0.9 m behind the wall and 0.8 m beyond the laser line for visual planning only. Support sizes are schematic. Beam graphics do not model reflections or establish laser clearances. Confirm the four-laser rig with ASC.
